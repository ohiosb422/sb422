Step 1 of 4

Testimony goes to every member of the committee

When a House committee holds a hearing on SB 422, anyone can submit testimony. It goes into the public record and to every member who will vote on the bill and its amendments. This page writes a clear, respectful statement for you in about ten minutes. You can change every word of it.

Why "interested party"?
ProponentOpponentInterested party

Ohio House committees take testimony as a proponent (for the bill), an opponent (against it), or an interested party. Interested party fits this request: you support what SB 422 is trying to do, and you're asking the committee to change one part of it, not to pass it as is or to defeat it.

How will you testify? *
Testimony is public. The House upload form says anything submitted may be released to the public and published online, and committee web pages list each witness by name beside their testimony. If SB 422 would restrict you, you don't have to say so; the next step lets you choose how to describe yourself. Leave out children's names, your street address, and anything you wouldn't want online.

    On iPhone, the download opens your share menu: choose Save to Files so you can upload it.

    How to submit it

      +Tips for testifying in person

      Arrive early, and bring a printed copy to read from. The chair calls witnesses by name.

      Begin by addressing the chair and members, as your testimony already does. Read at a steady pace; about two to three minutes is ideal. The chair may set a time limit, so don't be thrown if asked to summarize.

      If a member asks a question you can't answer, it's fine to say "I don't know, but I can follow up." You don't have to share anything more personal than what's in your written statement.

      Thank the chair when you finish. That's it.

      +Want someone to look it over first?

      Send your PDF or text to ohiosb422 [at] proton [dot] me. I'll read it, check that every statement about the bill is accurate, and help you get it in on time. Please allow a day before the deadline.
