Senate Bill 422136th General Assembly

Ask your Ohio House member to let schools say yes

Passed the Senate 33–0 on June 10, 2026 · now in the Ohio House
Please act before November 9. SB 422 is expected to be assigned to a House committee as early as November 9 or 10. The best time to fix the bill is before that committee starts its work. Once a bill moves forward, there are fewer chances to change it. If SB 422 passes as written, parents on Ohio's registry whom the bill restricts would be barred from their children's school events for as long as they remain registered: 25 years for Tier II, and for life for Tier III. Schools would have no way to make an exception. This takes about five minutes.

2Speak at the committee hearing, or send written testimony

When the House committee schedules a hearing on SB 422, members of the public may testify. You can speak in person at the Statehouse in Columbus, or submit written testimony without making the trip. Testimony and a witness form are due about 24 hours before the hearing. A clear, respectful two-minute statement from a parent, grandparent, teacher, or neighbor carries real weight.
